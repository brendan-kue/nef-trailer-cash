Privacy

What we collect

When you sign up and use NEF Trailer Cash we keep your name, email address and cellphone number, your coin balance and today's trailer ratings, and your withdrawal requests (method, payout details such as the cellphone or bank account you enter, amount and status).

Why

We use this only to run your account, count your coins and pay out your withdrawals. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Your account and data are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Trailers play from YouTube, which has its own privacy policy.
